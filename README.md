## ADHAYAYAN-Computer-Science-Student-Academic-Support-Website-with-AI-Assistance
## Short Description: 
Adhyayan is an AI-powered academic support website designed specifically for computer science students. It acts as a 24/7 personal tutor, providing instant assistance with complex theories, coding problems, and exam preparation to enhance understanding and academic success.


---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **🤖 अध्यYAN AI Chatbot:** Powered by the Google Gemini API to provide instant, human-like explanations for coding bugs and CS concepts.
* **📊 Interactive DSA Sheet:** A dedicated tracker for Data Structures and Algorithms.
    * **Topic-wise Organization:** Problems categorized by Arrays, Strings, etc.
    * **Local Storage:** Your progress (checkboxes) is saved automatically in your browser.
    * **External Links:** Direct access to LeetCode problem pages.
* **📂 Semester-wise Resources:** Organized YouTube playlists and handwritten notes (PDFs) for core subjects like DBMS, OS, and Networking.
* **🛤️ Educational Roadmaps:** Step-by-step guides for mastering various technology stacks.
* **📄 Professional Resume Templates:** Minimalist and Executive templates, including specialized formats for Amity placements.
* **📱 Fully Responsive:** Seamless experience across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile.

---

### Setup & AI Integration

To activate the अध्यYAN AI Assistant:

1. Get a free Google AI API key from: https://makersuite.google.com/app/apikey
2. Open the अध्यYAN AI page on the site.
3. Enter your API key in the setup prompt.
4. Note: Your key is stored locally in your browser for security.
